## Description

Every place on Earth has a flavor. Terroir lets you taste it.

Spin a 3D globe, tap any coastline, mountain, or city, and Terroir renders a flavor card for that exact spot: a radial chart across twelve dimensions (earthy, mineral, bright, citric, floral, herbaceous, smoky, woody, saline, tannic, vegetal, aromatic) with sommelier-style tasting notes, built from real soil, climate, vegetation, elevation, and coastal data.

**What you get:**
• A real 3D globe you can spin and zoom
• Tap any point on Earth for an instant flavor profile
• Twelve-dimension radial flavor chart with tasting prose
• Works fully offline from a bundled flavor grid
• No accounts, no tracking, no ads

A one-time purchase. No subscriptions.

## App Review Notes

```
Terroir is an offline reference app. Spin a 3D globe (SceneKit), tap any location,
and a flavor profile is computed from a bundled geographic data grid (FlatBuffers).
A one-time purchase unlocks the full experience; there are no subscriptions and no
accounts. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs beyond the FlatBuffers
runtime. Optional "More Detail" enrichment calls a CloudKit/Vercel endpoint; core
tap-to-flavor works fully offline.
```
